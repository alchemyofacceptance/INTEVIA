## Interpretation rules

- Indexing is not authority.
- A path's presence does not establish current applicability, ratification, or canon status.
- Working material must not be treated as ratified unless an exact Human-issued source establishes that state.
- Human ratification or acceptance is signalled only by the Human Governor's explicit word — "Accepted", or historically "This is the way of the HAT" — and by nothing else. AI agreement never constitutes a decision.
- Historical evidence remains truthful at issue.
- Human proposal, AI review, implementation, qualification, and final Human decision are distinct capacities.
- Documentation does not establish current test execution, implementation acceptance, release readiness, certification, or deployment.
