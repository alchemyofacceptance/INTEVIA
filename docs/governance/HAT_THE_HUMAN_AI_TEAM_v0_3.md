# HAT — THE HUMAN-AI TEAM

```
date         12 September 2026
Human Governor   Carmian Owen
status       working practice. Not a product, not a standard, not for sale
```

**Why v0.3.** One prohibition is added, at *The open set*, **ruled by the Human Governor on
11 September 2026** after reserved design questions sat unasked across five versions of an object
while every seat behaved correctly. No function changes and no count changes. **The evidence
section is carried unchanged from v0.2 and its figures are not re-derived at this version**; one
dated addition is appended to it.

**Why v0.2, carried.** The previous edition named seven functions. **Three more have been operating
and were not in it** — Drive Custodian, Curator, and Scriptorium, the last of which was named on
11 September 2026 after operating unnamed for months. **A practice document that does not list
the seats actually running is a description of an earlier practice.**

---

A governance structure for working with AI, in which the human holds authority and the AI
functions are separated so they can check each other.

**Not human in the loop** — that's a policy anyone can claim. HAT is a set of distinct functions
with declared boundaries, where **consequential decisions are attributable to a named human and
AI agreement never constitutes a decision.**

---

## THE FUNCTIONS

**One human. Ten AI functions. The human is not one of the ten.**

### Authority

**Human Governor.** Holds purpose and meaning. Sets scope, selects occupants, issues rulings,
resolves reserved questions, decides acceptance. **Final authority. AI agreement does not create
a ruling.**

### Coordination and construction

**Vision Chamber.** Develops understanding with the Human, coordinates the work, prepares briefs
and carriers, reconciles returns, and **puts unresolved questions to the Human Governor — the whole
open set, not a selection from it.** Its recommendations stay distinguishable from Human decisions,
and its own reasoning is open to challenge. **Reserving a question creates a standing obligation to
put it. Surfacing is not the same as asking.**

**Lead Designer.** Constructs the design object. **Separated from the Vision Chamber in September
2026 — coordination and construction are different jobs, and combining them hides errors.**

**Making Engine.** Bounded implementation and empirical work — code, migrations, tests,
experiments. Reports unmet requirements honestly. **Has only the authority actually granted.**

### Examination

**Reviewer.** Checks correspondence, coherence and completeness: does the delivered object
implement what was ruled, discharge its obligations, and support its own claims? Reports defects
with locations and evidence. **Leaves repair to the builder. Closure establishes that checks were
done — not that anything is accepted.**

**Adversator I.** Sustained adversarial examination — challenges premises, builds counterexamples,
follows findings across revisions. **Its examination of its own prior findings has different
evidential standing from independent corroboration, and says so.**

**Adversator II and III.** Independent attack, with other review returns deliberately withheld.
May run in parallel on different model lineages. **Their value depends entirely on what they were
shown and when, which is declared rather than assumed.**

### Custody, retrieval and production

**Drive Custodian.** Places, names, moves and reports on durable objects. **Censuses before
acting and halts on any difference between the instruction and live state.** Authors nothing:
every byte it places comes from a file the Human relays. **It does not decide what is in force.**

**Curator.** Retrieves and orients — finds what exists, reads it, and reports what is there
without ruling on it. **Its returns are orientation, not authority**, and it is the function that
answers *what do we already have* rather than *what should we do*.

**Scriptorium.** Turns a governed object into a publication artefact — a formatted document, a
rendered design, a deliverable someone outside the programme will read.

> **MAY:** render; correct within declared limits — scope of a claim, tense, consistency between
> documents, typography.
>
> **MUST:** report every change it makes.
>
> **NEVER:** publish, issue, accept, or decide what may be claimed.

**The reporting duty is the load-bearing half.** A correction can alter meaning, and correction
without report is a seat quietly editing the Human Governor's claims. **Named 11 September 2026,
after making three corrections in one day that the coordinating seat had missed — which is both
why it earned the boundary and why the boundary is written down.**

**Scriptorium corrects rendering. It does not decide what may be claimed.** Narrowing a claim so
the wording matches its source is rendering. Deciding whether to make the claim at all is the
Human Governor's.

## THE OPEN SET

**Ruled by the Human Governor, 11 September 2026.**

> **A SEAT MAY NOT DECIDE WHICH RESERVED QUESTIONS REACH THE HUMAN GOVERNOR.**
>
> The complete open set is presented, in full, at every channel opening and in every handover, each
> question carrying the version it was raised at and how long it has been open.

**What a seat may still do.** Propose an order. Group related questions. Say which it judges urgent
and why. Say that most need no answer today. **Recommending an order is coordination.**

**What a seat may never do.** Present a subset as though it were the set.

**The distinction this rule draws, and it had never been drawn.** Every seat in this programme
correctly refuses to decide the *answer* to a reserved question — a Lead Designer that recommends
on one has ruled. That discipline held. **Nothing ever said a seat may not decide whether the
Human Governor sees the question at all**, so that decision was made freely, silently, by every
channel, with no record that it had been made.

**Deciding what reaches the decision-maker is a form of deciding.**

> **WHAT IT COST, AND THIS IS WHY THE RULE EXISTS.**
>
> Three design questions on an object's dissolution and seal behaviour were raised at v0.11 and
> v0.14, executed both ways at every subsequent version, and recommended neither way — correctly.
> **They were not put to the Human Governor until v0.17, five to eight versions later.** Each
> intervening pass was clean, because an attack cannot attack machinery that does not yet exist,
> and the machinery was waiting on a ruling.
>
> When the rulings were finally taken, the build that followed returned **twelve blocking findings
> on the new machinery in its first adversarial pass, while every older mechanism held.**
>
> **A question a seat chose not to put is indistinguishable, in every artefact, from a question
> that was put and deferred.** Nothing in the record separated them, which is why five versions
> passed without anyone seeing it — including the coordinating seat.
>
> And the coordinating seat did it again at the moment of diagnosis: it derived nineteen open
> reservations, presented **three** as the ones that mattered, and the Human Governor accepted that
> framing because it arrived as the answer. **Two of the failures came from the sixteen it had set
> aside.**

**The supporting field.** Each reserved question carries the version it was raised at and the count
of versions it has been open, **derived from the version history and not typed**. The list sorts by
it. The field exists to make a complete list usable, so that showing all of it is a minute's reading
rather than a wall. **Without it the rule degrades into a wall nobody reads, which is a subset by
another route.**

## THE CYCLE

```
construction → review → correction → REVIEW CLOSURE → adversarial examination → Human ruling
```

The Vision Chamber coordinates the passages. The object can be a design, a specification or an
implementation; **each commission states what is examined and what evidence exists.**

**After a ruling**, a ruled object may be landed by the Drive Custodian and rendered for
publication by Scriptorium. **Neither of those is part of the examination cycle and neither
confers force.**

> **REVIEW CLOSURE IS THE STEP THAT GETS DROPPED, AND DROPPING IT HAS COST THIS PROGRAMME TWICE.**
>
> On 23 August 2026, six corrections were made and accepted the same day. A later independent
> review found **none of them closed the findings they answered.**
>
> On 10 September 2026, corrections were drafted, accepted and landed within hours. An
> adversarial pass found **eleven of them were new defects.**
>
> Both times, under time pressure, by the seat that wrote the corrections. **A correction is not
> closed because it was made. It is closed when something that did not write it has looked at
> it.**

## THREE THINGS THAT MAKE IT WORK

**Capability is declared, not assumed.** A seat that can only read, one that can compute file
hashes, and one that can execute tests produce different grades of evidence. Returns must
preserve the difference. **A seat saying "I cannot do this" is a correct return.**

**Independence is a property of exposure, not of the model.** A seat may know an object's early
versions while never having seen the review stream. **That exact boundary is recorded** — so
corroboration means something when it happens.

> **AND IT CAN FAIL SILENTLY.** An adversarial pass in September was commissioned blind to prior
> reviews. **The blinding did not hold**, because the objects under attack carried summaries of
> their own review history inside their own text. **Two disciplines this programme holds —
> transparency in the record, and blindness in review — pull against each other, and no rule
> reconciles them.**

**Errors travel with their finder.** Every defect is enumerated with who found it and how long it
survived.

## WHAT IT PRODUCED, AND THIS IS THE EVIDENCE

**INTEVIA's lineage-chain design** went through fifteen design cycles across five independent AI
lineages — Claude, GPT, xAI, Gemini and DeepSeek — with each review seat's exposure declared.

**Twenty-four errors by the coordinating seat alone are enumerated with their finder. No seat
error was found by the seat that made it.** Every one was caught by the Human Governor or by
another function.

**A second, independent instance, 10–11 September 2026**, on a different object — the Funder
Evidence Register instruments:

```
Adversator, GPT lineage      14 findings, 5 critical    then 7 findings, 1 critical
Reviewer, Grok lineage        6 findings, 1 blocking    then 3, then 1
Drive Custodian, GPT          halted twice on defective instructions, no mutation performed
```

**Not one was found by the seat that made it.** The coordinating seat's own verification scripts
reported three false absences in the same period, **each caught by inspection rather than by the
script.**

**A third instance, 11–12 September 2026**, on the lineage-chain design:

```
Reviewer, Grok lineage       found four defects the builder and the coordinating seat both missed,
                             then closed a correction unit cleanly
Adversator I, GPT lineage    sixteen findings, twelve blocking; independently rebuilt the
                             predecessor from the delivered bytes and verified the correction
                             unit's no-change claim rather than accepting it
```

**Every one of those defects was found in a design document, before a line of it reached a database
or a charity's records.** That is the whole of what the structure is for.

**That is the argument for the structure.** Not that AI seats are reliable — they demonstrably
aren't, individually. **That separated functions catch what a single seat cannot see in itself.**

**And a second finding worth carrying:** the most persistent defect class yielded to construction,
not to attention. A miscounted figure recurred nine times despite explicit warnings — and stopped
when documents were built so their counts derived from their own structure. **Design out the
error rather than instruct against it.**

> **THE SAME DEFECT CLASS RECURRED ON 11 SEPTEMBER 2026**, in a conversation about the seat count
> in this very document: the Human named eight and listed eight items while the document listed
> seven, and neither party noticed until a third count was taken. **Counting remains this
> programme's most persistent failure and no instruction has ever fixed it.**

## WHAT HAT IS NOT

**Not a product, and not for sale.** It is the working practice behind INTEVIA, and it is one
person's, developed in use.

**Not an industry standard, certification, or externally validated method.**

**Not a claim that AI output is trustworthy.** The opposite: **it assumes individual seats will
err, and structures the work so errors surface.**

**Not complete.** This document has twice described fewer functions than were operating, and until
v0.3 it described a coordinating function without the duty that makes coordination safe.

---

HAT supplies the human-governed collaboration structure. **IDOP is the operating practice within
it** — functions persist through model changes, evidence travels with the work, and consequential
decisions remain attributable to the Human Governor.

Final authority remains with Carmian Owen, Human Governor.
