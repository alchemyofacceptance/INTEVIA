# Ruling 109 · participation arithmetic

This clarification accompanies ruling 109 of the U21e/U21f unit record. At INTEVIA’s 72% CQ participation threshold, three eligible Stewards require three participants, four require three, and five require four.

| Eligible Stewards | 72% of eligible | Minimum whole participants |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 2.16 | 3 |
| 4 | 2.88 | 3 |
| 5 | 3.60 | 4 |

The calculation rounds up to the next whole person. With four eligible Stewards, three participating is 75%, which meets the 72% threshold. INTEVIA’s settings recorded in ruling 109 are quorum off, CQPT 72 and CQT 100. All who decide must agree.
