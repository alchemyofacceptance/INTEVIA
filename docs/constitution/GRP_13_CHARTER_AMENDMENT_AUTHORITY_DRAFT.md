# GRP-13 · proposed amendment on charter authority

**Draft awaiting Carmian Owen’s authorisation.** This is a proposal to amend the governed amendment cycle. It has no operational effect or LAW record. Package L carries it for review. Ratification of a future amendment, its commit and any downstream publication follow the existing cycle. [GRP-13 Governed Amendment Cycle](GRP_13_GOVERNED_AMENDMENT_CYCLE.md)

## Proposed addition

The change process for the Free Will Charter and the CARE Constitutional Charter requires an accountable decision by INTEVIA’s root. Before the Circle of Stewards functions, the effective Founder & Steward may authorise a charter change. If he has died or cannot act during that period, protected root changes are held and existing law stays in force.

The Circle of Stewards functions for this purpose when it has at least three members and CQ is built and active. Once it functions, the Circle proposes the charter change and the Founder & Steward authorises it. He may withhold authorisation. The functioning Circle authorises alone when he cannot act, after succession, or on his recorded handover. Record which route applied, its evidence, the proposed text and the authorising decision. Ruling 109 supplies INTEVIA’s participation settings. [Participation arithmetic](../governance/RULING_109_PARTICIPATION_CLARIFICATION.md)

The Emergency Steward office carries no authority to propose on behalf of the Circle or to authorise a charter change. No route ends either charter’s constitutional protection without an explicit ruling from the INTEVIA Founder & Steward. Preparation, ratification, adoption into LAW and downstream publication each keep their own evidence.

## Review boundary

The proposed mutation concerns the GRP-13 amendment cycle. Both ratified charter texts and the existing cycle retain their independent identities. The sources are the Human Governor’s package L addendum 4 of 5 November 2026 and his P3b ruling on death or inability before the Circle functions. The Human Governor reviews this draft and determines whether and how a subsequent change to the cycle may be committed. No amendment or publication is authorised by delivery of this draft.
