# Contributing to INTEVIA

Thank you for your interest in INTEVIA.

## Where things stand

We welcome issue reports, questions and ideas. For now the maintainers
do not merge outside code, tests, documentation or artwork into the
official repository. Please describe the problem or idea in your own
words. You retain the rights you have in your submission.

The project is preparing its review process and contributor terms so
that the rights in material it accepts can be traced. This policy
governs acceptance upstream. You can already fork INTEVIA, develop
changes independently and share them under the applicable licences.

## What you can do now

- Read, run, study, change and share INTEVIA. The licence gives you those
  rights.
- Report a problem, ask a question or suggest an idea, through this
  repository's issue tracker where it is open, or through the contact
  route in [NOTICE](NOTICE). Please describe it in words and leave code
  out.

## When contributions open

Before accepting changes, the maintainers will publish the review
process and the terms on which contributors can submit material they
have authority to contribute. Any permission beyond the applicable
open-source licence will be stated expressly for the contributor to
consider.
