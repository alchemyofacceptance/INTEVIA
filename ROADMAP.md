## Human decision gates

Exact Human authority is required before:

1. selecting or reordering programme priorities;
2. authorising a repository mutation, commit, or push;
3. changing a Slice, packet, or finding state;
4. accepting a packet or Slice, or closing one;
5. claiming deployment, release, external validation, productisation, or production readiness;
6. launching a Circle within an Organism, or populating an Organism;
7. activating HPCC certification or educator qualification; or
8. making a public funding or feasibility claim that exceeds cited evidence.

The mutation/commit/push gate is the programme's standard execution gate. It is not evidence that any direction candidate has been selected.
