## Agreed next work and longer-term direction

The C series (2 to 16 November 2026) carried out the C, R, Circles and decision-by-vote work of the 1 November re-plan; [its record](docs/holocron/datacrons/D-S015-C-SERIES-GOVERNED-CIRCLES-LAWS-DECISIONS-AND-TIME.md) lists what was built and, in §9, what remains owed.

1. **Alpha readiness is the agreed next work.** Its principal test case is a trustee's question about a vacant office, followed from the office's provision, through a discussion and a decision by vote, to what a later reader can see. The work joins the services already built to their first screens; connects the older LIBRARY, EVENTS and SERVICE domains to organisations; and prepares a local, disposable Alpha deployment.
2. Edge cases are recorded as owed work and brought in when their function is next worked on, so that ordinary use comes first. The C-series record §9 keeps each restriction and assignment explicit.

Later S015 work, wider product hardening, HPCC curriculum and educator formation, and future public, funding, cohort and deployment decisions remain subject to their own commissions and Human decisions. HPCC means HAT Practitioner Certification Curriculum. This document adds no scope or delivery deadline.

The September roadmap listed pre-PKT-B questions about identity, authority and history verification. That was the starting backlog. This refresh does not declare all of it closed; the Vision Chamber should reconcile any remaining finding against the current ledger before it is commissioned again.

## Human decision gates

Exact Human authority is required before:

1. selecting or reordering programme priorities;
2. authorising a repository mutation, commit, or push;
3. changing a Slice, packet, or finding state;
4. accepting a packet or Slice, or closing one;
5. claiming deployment, release, external validation, productisation, or production readiness;
6. launching a Circle within an Organism, or populating an Organism;
7. activating HPCC certification or educator qualification; or
8. making a public funding or feasibility claim that exceeds cited evidence.

The mutation/commit/push gate is the programme's standard execution gate. It is not evidence that any direction candidate has been selected.
